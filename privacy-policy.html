Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Business Notice: This privacy document outlines general standards regarding customer communications. Formal terms are subject to final business review by MJDELECTRONICS.

1. Information We Collect

MJDELECTRONICS collects information you voluntarily provide when inquiring about televisions, pricing, or local pickup arrangements. This typically includes your name, email address, telephone number, and message contents.

2. How We Use Information

Information submitted directly via phone, email, or marketplace messaging is used solely to:

  • Respond to your product availability and pricing inquiries.
  • Coordinate dates, times, and instructions for scheduled local pickup in Lithia Springs, GA.
  • Provide customer service regarding television operations and specifications.

3. Third-Party Marketplaces

If you interact with MJDELECTRONICS through third-party platforms such as OfferUp, Facebook Marketplace, Mercari, 5Miles, or Nextdoor, the collection and retention of your data on those platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms of service.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your contact information to third parties or marketing brokers. We implement reasonable administrative measures to ensure inquiry communications remain private.
