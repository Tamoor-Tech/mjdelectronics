Inspection & Return Terms

Last Updated: November 2026

Transparency Commitment: We prioritize in-person customer verification prior to purchase so buyers can examine screen clarity and functionality firsthand.

1. In-Person Pre-Purchase Inspection

At MJDELECTRONICS, we ensure that televisions are thoroughly pre-tested. When arranging local pickup in Lithia Springs, GA, customers are encouraged to inspect and verify:

  • Screen panel condition (no cracks, dead lines, or cosmetic display issues).
  • Power-on operation and backlighting brightness.
  • Included accessories such as remote control, power cable, and stands (where applicable).

2. Return & Exchange Terms

Because items are inspected and approved directly by the customer during scheduled in-person pickup, sales are typically considered final upon handover, unless specifically agreed otherwise in writing between the buyer and Melvin at the time of purchase.

(Any business-specific warranty or extended return policies remain subject to official confirmation and direct seller arrangement.)

3. Marketplace Transactions

Purchases completed through third-party platforms (such as OfferUp or Mercari) are subject to the specific buyer protection and dispute resolution policies enforced by that respective platform.
