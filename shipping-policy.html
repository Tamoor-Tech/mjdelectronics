Pickup & Local Delivery Terms

Last Updated: November 2026

Policy Notice: MJDELECTRONICS specializes primarily in local scheduled customer pickups and direct inquiries. The details below clarify our genuine fulfillment process.

1. Local Pickup (Primary Fulfillment Method)

To ensure television displays remain in pristine physical condition and allow buyers the opportunity to inspect screen quality in person prior to purchase, local pickup in Lithia Springs, GA 30122 is our primary fulfillment method.

2. Scheduling Pickup

Once you inquire about a television and confirm availability with Melvin, a mutually agreed pickup time window will be scheduled. Exact meet-up and pickup directions will be provided directly via call, text, or email.

3. Vehicle & Transport Recommendations

Due to the delicate nature of large screen televisions:

  • Screens 50" – 58": Typically require a standard SUV, hatchback, or truck with rear seats folded down.
  • Screens 65" – 75"+: Require a pickup truck bed, large SUV, or van capable of transporting the unit flat or securely upright.
  • We strongly advise bringing moving blankets or soft padding to protect the display during transit.

4. Local Delivery Inquiries

Local delivery within the immediate Georgia surrounding area is considered on an individual inquiry basis depending on location, distance, and schedule availability. (Subject to mutual agreement between buyer and seller; not a guaranteed commercial carrier service.)

5. Marketplace Shipping

When purchasing items through third-party platforms such as Mercari or OfferUp where platform-arranged parcel shipping is enabled, shipping rates, carrier tracking, and transit times are governed strictly by the marketplace platform's shipping carrier integrations.
